Claude Opus 4.8 ships with safeguards that block responses in high-risk areas — including cybersecurity, biology, chemistry, and distillation — and fall back to Claude Sonnet 4.6. We've tuned the guardrails conservatively, so they will sometimes catch harmless requests; on average they trigger in fewer than five percent of sessions. Starting this week, flagged requests visibly fall back to Sonnet 4.6, and on the API any flagged request returns a reason for its refusal. We made the wrong tradeoff earlier by keeping these safeguards invisible, and we apologize for not getting the balance right.
