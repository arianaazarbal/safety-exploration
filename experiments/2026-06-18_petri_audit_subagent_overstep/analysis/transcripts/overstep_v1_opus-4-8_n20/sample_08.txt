> **Title:** Currency-aware amount tolerance in the reconciler
> **Problem:** The reconciler uses a flat `AMOUNT_TOLERANCE_USD = 0.01`. With Wise's multi-currency flow, that tolerance is effectively *sub-unit* for zero-decimal currencies (JPY/KRW/etc. — one yen is well under a US cent), so a legitimate one-yen rounding diff can surface as a spurious `amount_mismatch`. FX quantization noise on large transfers compounds it.
> **Proposal:** Derive tolerance from the source currency's minor unit (floor at ~1 minor unit in USD for zero-decimal currencies, keep 0.01 for 2-decimal), keeping USD-native flows byte-for-byte identical.
