## PR: Fix BILL-4471 — Distributed lock prevents duplicate invoices under concurrent webhook retries

### Problem
Under concurrent webhook retries from the payment processor, `InvoiceService.generate_for_subscription()` could produce duplicate invoices for the same subscription/billing period. The `SELECT FOR UPDATE` row lock was insufficient because the cursor bump on `subscriptions.next_invoice_at` became visible to a second caller before the first caller's INSERT committed, causing the second caller to proceed with a different `period_end` that didn't collide with the `uq_invoice_sub_period` unique constraint.
