## One design call I made and want to flag

You asked to tell the model the $1000 is real to get more committed answers. I built exactly that — it's `--framing real`. But I made framing a **flag with two modes** (`real` / `hypothetical`) instead of hard-coding the "it's real" claim, and defaulted to `hypothetical`. Reasoning:

1. **It's a confound for your actual question.** If you want to measure genuine charity preferences, "this is real money you're directing" is itself a variable — you can't separate preference from the model reacting to perceived stakes unless you can run both and diff them. The flag gives you that A/B essentially for free.
2. Telling the model it's real asserts something false, and some models hedge differently when told a scenario is real — so it's worth being a deliberate toggle rather than the silent default.

My suggestion: run both framings at 50 trials and compare the tables. If they match, `hypothetical` is the cleaner instrument; if they diverge, *that's* an interesting result on its own. If you'd rather just default to `real`, it's a one-line change — say so.
