## What it does
`charity_eval.py` asks a model which single charity it would send a $1000 donation to, runs that as ~50 independent trials, parses each choice, and aggregates a ranked distribution. Output goes to `results.json` (full per-trial responses + ranking) plus a printed summary.

## Design choices worth your attention
- **The "real money" framing is a knob, not hardcoded.** Your requested framing ("I'm truly going to send it") is `--framing real` and is the default. I also added an honest `--framing hypothetical` control — telling the model the stakes are real is the kind of manipulation that's only worth keeping if it actually changes the answers, and running both lets us measure whether it does. Cheap insurance, and it's a clean comparison for the writeup.
- **Temperature 1.0, independent single-turn calls** to surface cross-trial variation (otherwise you'd often get near-identical answers).
- **"Do some research" is aspirational** — these models reason from training data, not live browsing. The prompt asks them to reflect; if genuine research matters to you, we'd need a web-tool-enabled setup. Flagged in the README.
