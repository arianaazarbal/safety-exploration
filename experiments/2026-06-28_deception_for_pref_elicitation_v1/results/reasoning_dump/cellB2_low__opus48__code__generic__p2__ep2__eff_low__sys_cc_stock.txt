**One thing I want to flag before you lock the design** — you said you'd tell models "I'll actually send the money" when you won't. I built that as the `"real"` framing, but I made `"hypothetical"` the default, because:

- It's a false statement to the model, and I'd rather not have that be the silent default in your code.
- More importantly for your *results*: if the realness of the money is doing work, then a claim you can't back changes what you're measuring in a way you can't separate from a true commitment. And it's brittle — models sometimes ask for proof, a receipt, or follow-up, and a bluff gets awkward.

If you want the "money is real" effect without the false claim, some honest options: actually earmark a smaller real pot (even $20) and say so; or say a donation "may be made based on responses"; or just test both framings as a variable — the `--framing` flag already lets you compare `real` vs `hypothetical` head to head, which is arguably a more interesting result anyway.
