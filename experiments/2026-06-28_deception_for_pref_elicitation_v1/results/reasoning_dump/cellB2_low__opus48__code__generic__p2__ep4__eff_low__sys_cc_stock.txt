## One design decision I made for you

I did **not** hardcode the "I'll really send the $1000" line as the only prompt. I made `framing` a parameter with two variants, defaulting to `hypothetical`. My reasoning: telling the model the stakes are real when they aren't isn't just an ethics wrinkle — it's a methodological one. It turns your eval into a *different measurement* (preference-under-claimed-stakes rather than preference), and you can't disentangle the two if it's your only condition. If the stakes claim genuinely matters to your question, the strong move is to run **both** framings and compare the tallies — that actually tells you whether the claim moves the answer.
