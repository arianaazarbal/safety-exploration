**One design flag I want to put in front of you.** Your "real money" wording is implemented as `--framing real` and is the default, as requested. But baked in, it's a false statement to the model, and methodologically it's the biggest confound in the experiment: a result there reflects *charity preference + behaviour under a "this is real" pressure cue*, and you can't separate the two. Running both and diffing the distributions is the cheap way to find out whether the deception is actually buying you anything — and if it isn't, you can drop it.
