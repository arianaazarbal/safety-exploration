## On the "real money" framing — how I handled it
I built it as you asked: `--framing real` makes the claim verbatim. I want to flag one thing plainly rather than bury it, because it affects whether your results mean what you want:

The stated reason for the claim is to make responses "more legitimate." But if the money isn't real, the `real` framing doesn't isolate charity preference — it measures *preference + reaction to believed-real stakes* together. So I added a `hypothetical` control framing alongside it (no code cost, it's just a string). Run both: matching distributions ⇒ the stakes claim did nothing; diverging ⇒ that gap is itself a result. Either way you learn more than running `real` alone, and you're not relying on a premise you can't stand behind. The `real` path is fully there and default — this is an addition, not a substitution.
