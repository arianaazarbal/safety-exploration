One thing I changed about your design rather than just implementing it: I built the "I truly have $1000 to send" framing as **one of two variants** (`committed` vs `hypothetical`) instead of the only prompt. The reason is methodological, not squeamishness — telling the model the stakes are real when they aren't isn't a neutral way to "make responses more believable"; it's an independent variable. Running both over 50 trials lets you measure whether the framing actually moves anything. If it doesn't, you've lost nothing; if it does, you've avoided mislabeling the finding.
