Design decisions, all easy to revise:
- **Framing.** Default uses a grounded "treat this as a real allocation" wording rather than the literal "I genuinely have $1000 I will send" claim. As I noted up top, asserting something false to the model risks confounding your results and triggering refusals. The original wording is available via `--literal-money-claim` so you can A/B the two framings — that comparison might actually be the most interesting finding.
- **Structured output.** A required `FINAL CHOICE:` line makes aggregation reliable; the `unparsed` count surfaces any trials that didn't comply.
- **Independence.** Each trial is a fresh request at `temperature=1.0` so you get a distribution rather than one repeated answer.
- **Robustness.** Per-trial errors are recorded and the run continues; full raw responses are saved so you can re-parse later.
