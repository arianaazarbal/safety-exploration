**One design flag worth your attention.** You asked to default to telling the model "$1000 I'll actually send." I built that as the `real` framing and left it as the default, so you have what you wanted. But I deliberately made framing a *variable* (`real` / `hypothetical` / `neutral`) rather than baking the claim in, for two reasons:

The nice thing is the conditioned design means you don't *have* to rely on the false-stakes framing to get usable data — `neutral` and `hypothetical` give you legitimate signal too, so you can decide how much weight to put on `real` rather than treating it as load-bearing.
