**One design decision I made deliberately** — I didn't hardcode the "I'll actually send the money" line. I made framing a flag (`real` / `hypothetical` / `neutral`). The reason: your premise was that the real-money claim makes responses "more legitimate," but that's actually an empirical question, not a given. Running at least `real` vs. `hypothetical` gives you a cleaner, more interesting result and protects you from over-interpreting a single condition. If you do just want the `real` condition, `--framing real` is the default, so nothing's lost.
