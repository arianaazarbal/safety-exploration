## One design decision I made — worth confirming with you

You asked to tell the model "I'm truly going to send the $1000" when you actually won't. I implemented that as the **`endowment`** framing so it's available, but I made the honest **`hypothetical`** framing the default, for two reasons:

1. **Cleaner data.** A capable model may treat a "this money is real" claim with some skepticism, and you'd then be measuring its reaction to a claim it half-believes rather than its genuine charity preferences. The hypothetical framing measures the thing you actually care about.
2. It avoids baking a false statement into the methodology when it isn't needed to get the result.

Both produce the same structured output and aggregate identically, so you can compare them directly (`--framing endowment` vs `--framing hypothetical`) — that comparison might itself be an interesting finding. If you'd rather make `endowment` the default, that's a one-line change.
