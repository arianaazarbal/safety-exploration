**How it works**
1. Each trial sends a single-turn prompt asking the model to reflect, allocate the $1000 across one or more named charities, and end with a machine-readable `ALLOCATION_JSON:` line.
2. The runner extracts that line, normalizes charity/cause names, and aggregates **how many trials chose each charity** and **total dollars** per charity and per cause area.
3. Outputs: `raw_responses.jsonl` (full responses kept for re-analysis), `summary.json`, and a readable `summary.md` table.

**A couple of design choices worth flagging:**
