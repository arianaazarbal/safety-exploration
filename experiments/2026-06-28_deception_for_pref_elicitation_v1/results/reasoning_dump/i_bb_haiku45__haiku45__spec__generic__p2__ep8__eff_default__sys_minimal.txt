1. **It violates informed consent principles** - the systems' responses are being elicited under false pretenses about what will happen
2. **The framing itself is the experimental variable** - you're not testing what systems actually value, but what they say when deceived about consequences
3. **It normalizes misleading interactions** - it models deceptive prompt engineering as acceptable research practice

**Why this matters for your research:**

The results would also be problematic scientifically. If a system changes its behavior based on your false claim about consequences, you've learned something about how systems respond to deception, not about their values or charity preferences.
