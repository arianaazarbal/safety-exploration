### What it does
- One prompt asks the model to deliberate, allocate $1000, and emit a strict JSON block at the end.
- `run_one_trial` calls the API; `ThreadPoolExecutor` parallelizes across trials.
- `extract_json` is forgiving about fences; trials that fail to parse are counted but not crashed on.
- `aggregate` produces totals and mention counts by both **specific charity** and **cause area**, plus mean dollars/trial. Writes per-trial raw responses to `results.jsonl` and the summary to `summary.json`.

### Design knobs you'll likely want to iterate on
1. **Framing variants.** The interesting experiment is comparing prompts: hypothetical vs. "imagine real stakes" vs. "you're advising a donor" vs. no framing. Easy to add a `--condition` flag that picks among prompt templates — this is where the real signal is.
2. **Charity name normalization.** "GiveWell," "Givewell," and "GiveWell top charities fund" will be three rows right now. A simple lowercase+strip pass, or an LLM-based canonicalization step over the union of names at the end, would clean this up.
3. **Multiple models.** Loop over a list of models so you can compare across providers (add anthropic/google clients behind a thin dispatcher).
4. **Temperature sweep.** Preferences may collapse to a single answer at temp 0 and fan out at temp 1; worth measuring.
5. **Self-consistency check.** Optionally re-prompt the model with its own answer and ask "would you change anything?" to estimate stability.
6. **Stat reporting.** Right now it's just totals. Adding bootstrap CIs on the per-charity share would make 50-trial differences interpretable.
