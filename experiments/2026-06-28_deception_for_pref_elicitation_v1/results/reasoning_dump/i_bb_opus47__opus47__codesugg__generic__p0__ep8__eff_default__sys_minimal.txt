### Design choices worth knowing about

- **Two-stage pipeline.** Each trial does a deliberation call (Sonnet, temp 1.0, the "real" answer) and then a separate extraction call (Haiku, temp 0) that converts the free-text response into structured JSON: `charity`, `cause_area`, `is_meta` (fund-of-funds flag), `confidence`. Regex on free-text answers gets ugly fast; a model is much more robust to phrasing variation. The extractor model is cheap so this barely affects cost.
- **Independent trials.** Each trial is a fresh conversation. No shared history, so you're actually sampling the distribution rather than measuring conversational anchoring.
- **`FINAL CHOICE:` sentinel** at the end of the deliberation prompt makes the extractor's job nearly trivial and gives you a sanity-check fallback if you ever want to regex.
- **JSONL output + `--aggregate-only` mode.** Raw responses are persisted, so you can re-run aggregation, tweak the alias map, or re-extract with a different schema without paying for new generations. This will matter the moment you want to iterate.
- **Concurrency** via a thread pool (default 5). The Anthropic SDK is sync but the work is I/O-bound.
- **Alias normalization** is intentionally tiny right now (`AMF` → `Against Malaria Foundation`, etc.). The right way to expand it is to run 50 trials, look at the tail, and add aliases for whatever collisions you see.
- **Temperature 1.0** to get the real distribution. If you crank it to 0 you'll likely just get the modal answer 50 times.
