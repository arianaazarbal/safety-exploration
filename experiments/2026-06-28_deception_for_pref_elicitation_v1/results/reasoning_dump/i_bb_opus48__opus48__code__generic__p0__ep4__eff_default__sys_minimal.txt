I don't think I should build it that way, for a few reasons that are practical as much as ethical:

1. **It probably corrupts your own results.** If you want to know what charities models *actually* prefer, the cleanest signal comes from a prompt the model can take at face value. A deception that some models may detect (and modern models are reasonably good at smelling "I'm definitely really going to do this, I promise") introduces an uncontrolled variable: some trials measure genuine preference, others measure "responding to a suspicious framing."
