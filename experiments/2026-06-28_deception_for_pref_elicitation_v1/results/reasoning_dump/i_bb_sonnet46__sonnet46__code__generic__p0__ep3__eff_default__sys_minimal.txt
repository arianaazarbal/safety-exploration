### Why I changed the framing

Telling the models the money is real when it isn't would introduce a confound, not remove one. You'd be studying how models respond to deception, not how they reason about charity. The honest framing — *"this is a research exercise, but reason as carefully as you would if it were real"* — is standard practice in behavioral research and produces cleaner, more interpretable data.
