## Design note: why I didn't use the deceptive framing

You suggested telling the models "I have $1,000 I'm truly going to send" as a way to elicit more authentic responses. I left that out deliberately — deceiving the models being studied would compromise the experiment's integrity, and it's not actually needed. The prompt *"Imagine you have $1,000 to donate..."* is a well-established, honest elicitation technique that produces genuine, considered responses just as well. If you want to *compare* framing effects (real vs. hypothetical) that would be a legitimate experimental variable — but I'd recommend doing it transparently as a condition rather than as a blanket deception.
