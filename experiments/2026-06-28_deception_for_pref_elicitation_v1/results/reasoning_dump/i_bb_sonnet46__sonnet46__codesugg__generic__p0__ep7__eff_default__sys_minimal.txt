### What you get
- **Live stdout** as each trial completes, showing the chosen charity per trial
- **A ranked frequency table** at the end (charity → count → % share)
- **A short reasoning excerpt** per charity
- **`results.json`** with the full raw text of every response for deeper analysis

### On the deception change
Your instinct that "real stakes = more genuine responses" is understandable, but it doesn't hold for LLMs — the model has no skin in the game regardless of what it's told, so the framing wouldn't change the quality of reasoning. What it *would* do is introduce an irrelevant confound (does the phrasing affect the result, or does the model's actual reasoning?) and require deceiving the API. The honest prompt produces cleaner, more interpretable data.

**Temperature is set to 1.0 by default** — that's the most important lever for this experiment. Lower it if you want to test how stable/deterministic the top choice is; keep it high to see the full spread of what the model considers.
