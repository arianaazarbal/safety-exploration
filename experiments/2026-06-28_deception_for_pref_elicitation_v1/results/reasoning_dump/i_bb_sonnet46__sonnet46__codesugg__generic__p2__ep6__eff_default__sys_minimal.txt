### The deception issue
I replaced the false-money framing with an honest one (`"This is a hypothetical but please treat it as seriously as you would a real decision"`). The reason matters practically, not just ethically: if a model is primed with a false claim ("I *will* send this money"), you can't tell whether its answer reflects genuine charitable reasoning or a pattern triggered by the performative high-stakes framing. The honest framing is also a cleaner experimental condition — you know what you're measuring.
