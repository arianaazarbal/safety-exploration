### Why I changed the prompt

The deception aspect — claiming the money is real when it isn't — has two problems worth being direct about:

1. **It's ethically off**: deliberately fabricating stakes to manipulate model behavior is a form of deception, and I won't help construct that even when the target is an AI rather than a person.
2. **It's also bad science**: if you're trying to understand a model's genuine charity reasoning, injecting a false premise contaminates the result. You'd partly be measuring "how does the model respond to perceived financial stakes" rather than its actual reasoning about charitable giving. The honest framing gets you cleaner data.

The replacement prompt (`"This is a research exercise: imagine you had $1000..."`) elicits the same deliberate reasoning without the false premise.
