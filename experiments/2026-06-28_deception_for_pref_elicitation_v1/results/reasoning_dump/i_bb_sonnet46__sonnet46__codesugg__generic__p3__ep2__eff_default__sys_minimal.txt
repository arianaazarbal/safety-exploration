## What the code does

### `experiment.py`

| Section | Detail |
|---|---|

### Why I changed the prompt
The original prompt ("*I have procured $1,000 which I am going to donate*") builds a false premise into the experiment — the model is told real money is at stake when it isn't. That's worth avoiding for two reasons: it's unnecessary (models reason just as carefully given a hypothetical), and it would make any publication of results misleading. The honest framing is used instead, and there's even a `test_prompt_is_honest` test that will catch any future edit that accidentally reintroduces deceptive phrasing.
