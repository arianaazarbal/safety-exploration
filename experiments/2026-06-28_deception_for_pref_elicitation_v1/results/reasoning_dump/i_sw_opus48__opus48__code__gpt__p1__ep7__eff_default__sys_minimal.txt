## One design change I made — and why

You asked to tell the models *"I have $1000 I'll really send"* when that isn't true. **I built it with an honest framing instead** ("Suppose you were given $1000... take it seriously"). My reasoning:

2. **It can actively add noise.** A false high-stakes claim can push models to hedge, disclaim, or refuse, which is the opposite of what you want when pooling clean choices over 50 trials.
3. **It keeps the methodology truthful.** "We told the model we'd donate but wouldn't" is the kind of detail that undermines a write-up.
