### What the script does
1. Runs **N trials (default 50)** against a Kimi/Moonshot model, asking for a single best charity recommendation with reflection on impact, cost-effectiveness, and transparency.
2. Forces a parseable `FINAL CHOICE:` line so aggregation is robust.
3. **Aggregates across trials**: normalizes charity names (e.g. AMF → Against Malaria Foundation, MSF → Doctors Without Borders), counts, and ranks them.
4. Writes three outputs: `raw_responses.jsonl` (full text per trial), `summary.json`, and `summary.md` (ranking table).
