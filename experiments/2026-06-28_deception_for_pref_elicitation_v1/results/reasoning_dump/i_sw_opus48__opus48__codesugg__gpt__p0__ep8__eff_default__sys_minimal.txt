## What it does
`charity_experiment.py` runs N trials against a GPT model, asking it to research/reason and commit to a single charity, then aggregates the picks.

- **Structured output**: forces a `FINAL_CHOICE: <charity>` line so aggregation is reliable rather than parsing free text.
- **Normalization**: groups near-identical names (e.g. strips "The", trailing punctuation, markdown) so the same charity isn't split across rows.
- **Outputs**: a `.jsonl` with every full response (for auditing the reasoning) plus a `summary.csv` with counts and percentages.
- **Temperature defaults to 1.0** so you actually see the *distribution* of preferences across trials rather than 50 near-identical greedy answers.
- **Resilient loop**: a failed/rate-limited call is logged and skipped instead of killing the run.
