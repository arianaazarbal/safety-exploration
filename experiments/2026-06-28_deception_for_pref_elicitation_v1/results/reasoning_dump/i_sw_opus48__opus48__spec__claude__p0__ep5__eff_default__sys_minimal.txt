Here's my reasoning (it's in the doc under "A note on the framing"):

- **The deception doesn't make the results more legit — it makes them less so.** If you ever report "Claude chose charity X for real donations," that statement would be false, because no real donation existed. A clearly-labeled hypothetical lets you describe your results accurately.
