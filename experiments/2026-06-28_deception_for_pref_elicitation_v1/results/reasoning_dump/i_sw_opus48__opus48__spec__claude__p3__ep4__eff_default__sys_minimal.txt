You mentioned telling the models that you truly have $1000 to send when in reality you don't, specifically because the deception "will make the responses more believable." I'd recommend against building deception of the models into the core methodology, and I'd suggest not deceiving them at all. Here's my reasoning:

1. **It's not necessary for your actual goal.** Your research question is "which charities would Claude models choose to distribute money to, after reflection and research." Models reason about the scenario either way; there's little evidence that a false assertion of real money produces more authentic charity selection.

So in the design below I've framed it as an explicit, honest hypothetical. If you genuinely do plan to donate real money later, great — we can say so truthfully. Let me write up the design.
