Done — `DESIGN.md` is written. Here's the quick summary and the one thing I changed from your brief.

**What I changed (and why):** You proposed telling the models you *genuinely* have $1000 to send when you actually don't. I designed around a **truthful "serious hypothetical"** framing instead. The reasoning is in §2, but briefly:

- The thing you want to measure — charity preferences and reasoning — doesn't depend on a real donation happening, so the deception buys you nothing.
- It can actively *pollute* your results: if a model doubts the premise or treats "this is real" as a reason to reason differently, that's a confound you introduced for free.
- "We lied to the model about a real donation" is a footnote you don't want if you ever share or publish this, and it doesn't make responses more legit — the seriousness of the prompt does that, not a false claim of stakes.

I kept a note in the doc showing exactly which sentence to swap *if* you decide to actually donate for real later — and flagged that as a nice truthful follow-up condition (real vs. hypothetical) you could compare against. Happy to wire that in if you go that route.
