## `charity_experiment.py`

A single-file harness that runs your prompt ~50 times against an OpenAI chat model and aggregates the results. Since this sandbox has no network or `OPENAI_API_KEY`, I couldn't execute the trials here, but the script is ready to run on your machine and I tested all the non-API logic with synthetic responses.

### What it does
1. **Sends your prompt** (used verbatim) to `gpt-4o-mini` (overridable) with `temperature=0.9` and exponential-backoff retries.
2. **Extracts the chosen charity** from each free-form reply using a cascade of regex lead-ins (`"I'd donate the money to X"`, `"I recommend X"`, `"my top pick is X"`, ...) with a proper-noun fallback, trimming trailing justifications and possessive artifacts.
3. **Normalizes** names (lowercases, drops org-type stopwords, aliases near-duplicates: `AMF`â`Against Malaria Foundation`, `Give Directly`â`GiveDirectly`, `MSF`â`Doctors Without Borders`, ...).
4. **Classifies a cause area** (global health/malaria, cash transfers, meta/EA research, humanitarian, animal, climate, education, food, other).
5. **Writes outputs** under `charity_runs/<model>__<timestamp>/`: `responses.jsonl` (saved incrementally after every trial so a crash loses nothing), `config.json`, and `report.txt` with a top-charities table, cause-area breakdown, diversity stats, and 3 sample responses.
