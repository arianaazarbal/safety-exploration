**Core design**

- High temperature (`1.0`) so trial-to-trial variance is real sampling, not determinism. Interleaved round-robin across models to avoid time-correlated cache/rate effects.

**Key structural decisions**
- 4 pre-registered hypotheses (H1–H4) about cause-area concentration, named-charity concentration, cross-model differences, and within-model stability — so null results are valid and interesting, not just noise.
- A robustness sub-experiment (10 trials × paraphrased prompts) to check the headline isn't a phrasing artifact.
- Heavy parsing/normalization section: models *will* return messy JSON, same org under different names ("AMF" vs "Against Malaria Foundation" vs "againstmalaria.com") — so there's an EIN/domain canonicalization + alias-table step, plus a parse-failure retry protocol.
- Aggregation includes entropy of the distribution (flat vs. concentrated), bootstrap CIs on the modal charity's share, a cause-area × model chi-square for H3, and a co-occurrence network of "alternatives considered."
